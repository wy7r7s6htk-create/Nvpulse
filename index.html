Nvidia reports Q3 fiscal 2027 on 17 November: what to watch on the call

Nvidia reports results for the quarter ended 25 October 2026 after the US market close on 17 November. Analysts expect revenue of about $108.8 billion to $109 billion and earnings of $2.47 a share. The call starts at 2 pm Pacific (5 pm Eastern).

The analysis below was written before the results. The news lists further down update automatically from seven source groups.

The numbers going in

ItemFigure
Q2 FY2027 revenue (reported 26 Aug)$96.2 billion, up 18% on the quarter and 106% on a year earlier
Q2 data center revenue$89.0 billion, up 117% on the year

Analyst consensus, Q3 revenue$108.8 billion to $109 billion
Analyst consensus, Q3 earnings per share$2.47

Revenue against the guide

Consensus sits slightly above Nvidia’s own guide, so a small beat is already expected. The question is how large it is and what the fourth-quarter guide says about demand.

Rubin and Blackwell

Nvidia said last quarter that it built inventory ahead of the Vera Rubin launch in the third quarter, and that Blackwell was still the vast majority of revenue. Analysts will look for the first real Rubin revenue.

Margins and memory costs

Consensus for data center gross margin in fiscal 2027 is about 73.7%, below the 78% of fiscal 2024 and 2025. Higher memory prices could be passed on to buyers of systems and cloud capacity in 2027.
